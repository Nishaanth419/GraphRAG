Step 3: Cluster
Louvain finds groups of nodes more connected to each other than to the rest. Colors below are these communities.
Step 4: Summarize
Each community gets a text summary (an LLM writes it in full GraphRAG). Global questions search these summaries.

4. Scoreboard (word-count vs embeddings)

QuestionTypeRAG recallGraphRAG recallRAG + embeddingsGraphRAG + embeddingsGraphRAG on Neo4jRAG precisionGraphRAG precision

First two recall columns use word-count (TF-IDF) retrieval and exact entity-name matching; the "+ embeddings" columns use a local sentence-embedding model for both RAG and GraphRAG (entity linking and community ranking). Recall: share of the needed documents retrieved. Precision: share of retrieved documents that were needed. GraphRAG buys recall on multi-hop questions and pays with precision (more noise) and a costly indexing step.
